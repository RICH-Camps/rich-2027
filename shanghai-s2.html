The RICH Summer

Imagine tomorrow in Shanghai. Step into the stories of Chang’an.

在上海遇见未来，在长安走进故事

Ask how a robot learns a movement, look across Shanghai’s skyline and imagine a small invention that could make everyday life better. Then meet your Beijing campmates in Xi’an: stretch noodles with a chef, shape a little warrior and give a shadow puppet its first Chinese line. With teachers guiding the questions and the hands-on discoveries, children explore how people build, create and live—and find stories they are excited to tell for themselves.

机器人怎样学会一个动作？站在上海的高处，会怎样想象未来的城市生活？带着好奇走进科技与街巷，再到西安和北京营伙伴相遇，跟师傅拉一碗面、捏一个小俑人，让亲手做的皮影说出第一句中文。老师陪孩子问出问题、动手尝试，也在新朋友的回应里，找到越来越多想亲自讲述的中国故事。

The RICH Method · 学过的中文，带到生活里

Before every outing, teachers demonstrate and rehearse a language quest, then guide students at their own level. Beginners start with observation and prompt cards; more experienced speakers follow up, compare and explain. One-to-one Chinese tutoring—a RICH commitment for over a decade—gives each child time to practise with a teacher and make an expression their own.

每次外出都有中文任务。老师先示范、陪练，再按水平带队实践：初学者从观察和词句卡起步，已有基础的孩子尝试追问、比较和说明理由。坚持十余年的一对一中文辅导，让每个孩子都有和老师细细打磨表达的时间。

A question asked with a little more courage can become a conversation, and a conversation can become a friendship. Through a handmade piece, a shared meal or a story told in their own words, children find a sense of belonging in another culture—and moments of beauty to enjoy together, whatever language they grew up speaking.

勇敢问出的一个问题，可能成为一段对话、一份友谊。一件亲手做的作品、一张围坐的餐桌，让陌生的文化渐渐有了亲切感。孩子把所见所感写进笔记、讲成故事，也发现：即使说着不同的母语，总有值得一起欣赏的美。

Around our table · 尝新，也有熟悉的味道

A chef-led xiaolongbao class, a dining-hall meal with university students and a taste of Jiangnan beside the canals make food part of getting to know Shanghai. Japanese, Korean and Western meals add familiar favourites. In Xi’an, make your own biangbiang noodles and share Shaanxi flavours with new friends, discovering the people and patient craft behind a memorable meal.

小笼包师傅亲自带教，大学生陪大家到食堂选餐，水乡的一桌家常菜又打开江南的另一种滋味。日餐、韩餐与西餐穿插安排，让孩子尝新，也吃得熟悉。到了西安，亲手拉一碗biangbiang面，再和新伙伴分享陕西风味，把食物背后的手艺与人情一起记住。

Menus, seasoning and meal order are thoughtfully adapted to the day’s activities, students’ tastes and dietary needs.

具体菜品、辣度与用餐顺序，会结合当天活动、孩子的口味与饮食需求灵活安排。

小笼包非遗解码局带着课堂准备的问题向师傅请教，试做一个步骤；从食材名称到“先、再、最后”，把学到的手艺说给伙伴听。

DAY 08SUN27JUN

Humanoid Robots & Our Future Companions

人形机器人与未来伙伴

Morning上午

Learn how to describe an action, give a clear instruction and ask how something works in Chinese. Try a short sequence with a classmate and notice what happens when a direction needs to be more precise.

中文课上练习描述动作、发出清楚的指令，也学着追问“它是怎么做到的”。先和同学试一组动作，看看怎样把一句话说得更准确，让对方听懂。
