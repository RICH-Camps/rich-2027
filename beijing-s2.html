The RICH Summer

Step into China’s stories—and make them your own.

走进两座古都，成为故事里的人

Create a wuxia scene with friends above Beijing’s Central Axis. Watch the Great Wall emerge in the evening lights, then travel to Xi’an to shape a small warrior, dress in hanfu and give a shadow puppet a voice. With teachers helping each child find their words, history becomes something to enter, question and create together. Children return with a character they played, a piece they made and a story they can tell for themselves.

在景山和伙伴演一段武侠故事，在长城夜色里感受古都，再到西安亲手制作小俑人、换上汉服，让自己做的皮影开口说话。老师陪孩子练好台词、勇敢接话，也听他们说出自己的理解。两座古都里，有孩子扮演的角色、亲手做的作品，还有回家后能讲给家人听的：“这一段，是我们的故事。”

The RICH Method · 学过的中文，带到生活里

Before every outing, teachers demonstrate and rehearse a language quest, then guide students at their own level. Beginners start with observation and prompt cards; more experienced speakers follow up, compare and explain. One-to-one Chinese tutoring—a RICH commitment for over a decade—gives each child time to practise with a teacher and make an expression their own.

每次外出都有中文任务。老师先示范、陪练，再按水平带队实践：初学者从观察和词句卡起步，已有基础的孩子尝试追问、比较和说明理由。坚持十余年的一对一中文辅导，让每个孩子都有和老师细细打磨表达的时间。

A question asked with a little more courage can become a conversation, and a conversation can become a friendship. Through a handmade piece, a shared meal or a story told in their own words, children find a sense of belonging in another culture—and moments of beauty to enjoy together, whatever language they grew up speaking.

勇敢问出的一个问题，可能成为一段对话、一份友谊。一件亲手做的作品、一张围坐的餐桌，让陌生的文化渐渐有了亲切感。孩子把所见所感写进笔记、讲成故事，也发现：即使说着不同的母语，总有值得一起欣赏的美。

Around our table · 尝新，也有熟悉的味道

Peking duck, dumplings made with our Beijing hosts and biangbiang noodles prepared with a Xi’an chef bring local traditions to the table. Conveyor-belt sushi, Korean barbecue and Western favourites add variety. On our PKU campus day, dinner with university students gives children a taste of everyday campus life and another chance to get to know new friends.

尝一卷北京烤鸭，和老北京人家一起包饺子，再跟西安师傅学做biangbiang面；旋转寿司、韩式烤肉与西式餐桌穿插其间，让一路的口味更丰富。北大校园日，和大学生一起选餐、围坐交流，在一顿平常的食堂晚餐里，走近他们的大学生活。

Menus and meal order are thoughtfully adapted to the day’s activities, students’ tastes and dietary needs.

具体菜品与用餐顺序，会结合当天活动、孩子的口味与饮食需求灵活安排。
