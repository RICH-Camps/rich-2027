The RICH Summer

From future tech to panda care, discover more together.

Ask how a robot learns to move and discover the ideas behind a smart car. With teachers beside you, curiosity becomes a question you can ask in Chinese, something new to try and a discovery to share with a friend.

The RICH Method · 学过的中文，带到生活里

Before every outing, teachers demonstrate and rehearse a language quest, then guide students at their own level. Beginners start with observation and prompt cards; more experienced speakers follow up, compare and explain. One-to-one Chinese tutoring—a RICH commitment for over a decade—gives each child time to practise with a teacher and make an expression their own.

每次外出都有中文任务。老师先示范、陪练，再按水平带队实践：初学者从观察和词句卡起步，已有基础的孩子尝试追问、比较和说明理由。坚持十余年的一对一中文辅导，让每个孩子都有和老师细细打磨表达的时间。

A question asked with a little more courage can become a conversation, and a conversation can become a friendship. Through a handmade piece, a shared meal or a story told in their own words, children find a sense of belonging in another culture—and moments of beauty to enjoy together, whatever language they grew up speaking.

勇敢问出的一个问题，可能成为一段对话、一份友谊。一件亲手做的作品、一张围坐的餐桌，让陌生的文化渐渐有了亲切感。孩子把所见所感写进笔记、讲成故事，也发现：即使说着不同的母语，总有值得一起欣赏的美。

Around our table · 尝新，也有熟悉的味道

Make dumplings with a Beijing family, wrap your own Peking duck pancake and discover Sichuan flavours with new friends in Chengdu. Conveyor-belt sushi, Korean grilled beef and Western favourites bring variety to the table. A dining-hall meal with PKU students offers a glimpse of university life, with conversations that carry on beyond the campus walk.

和胡同人家一起包饺子，亲手卷一张烤鸭薄饼，再与成都的新伙伴分享川味。旋转寿司、韩式烤牛肉和西式餐桌穿插其间，尝新也有熟悉的滋味。北大校园日，和大学生一起去食堂选餐，让校园里的话题接着在餐桌边聊下去。

Menus, seasoning and meal order are thoughtfully adapted to the day’s activities, students’ tastes and dietary needs.

具体菜品、辣度与用餐顺序，会结合当天活动、孩子的口味与饮食需求灵活安排。

Beihai by Boat & Stories Around Shichahai

北海泛舟与什刹海的北京故事

Morning上午

A Chinese assessment helps teachers place you in a class that fits. In the first three-hour lesson, practise describing a place and asking about everyday life, ready to notice the people and stories beside Beijing’s lakes.

通过中文水平测试，进入适合自己的班级。第一堂3小时中文课里，练习介绍一处风景、询问日常生活，准备带着好奇去认识北京湖畔的人与故事。

After lunch on campus and a short rest, work on homework during one-to-one Chinese tutoring. Your teacher helps you practise a lakeside description and a question you would like to ask, with words and prompts suited to your level.

校内午餐、短休后，在一对一中文辅导中完成作业。和老师练练介绍湖畔风景的词句，再准备一句想问的问题，带着适合自己程度的表达去探索。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Take a boat onto Beihai and see the White Dagoba from the water, then explore the lanes around Shichahai with your teachers. Notice people fishing, walking and chatting, and ask a willing local about a small detail of lakeside life.

乘船游北海，从水面看白塔与湖岸，再随老师漫步什刹海。留意散步、闲聊的北京人，找一个自己好奇的生活细节，在老师陪伴下与愿意交流的人聊几句。

Language Quest今日中文任务

The Lakeside Life ReporterChoose one detail that makes this feel like Beijing. Begin with a word and a picture, or ask a local a question and follow up; bring a small story back to your group.

湖畔生活观察员找一个让你觉得“这很北京”的细节。可以用图片和词语记录，也可以向当地人提问、追问，再把听到的小故事分享给伙伴。

At the table餐桌风味

Beijing Copper-Pot HotpotAfter a lakeside afternoon, gather around a Beijing copper hotpot. Share thinly sliced meat, vegetables and sesame dipping sauce, compare favourite combinations and let the conversation wander through the little stories you have collected.

DAY 03THU8JUL

Hutong Hosts, Handmade Crafts & Our Dumpling Table

胡同人家的手艺与一桌饺子

Morning上午

In Chinese class, practise asking a host to show you how something is made. Name a material, describe a step and talk about a food from home, preparing to learn a traditional craft and share a kitchen with new friends.

中文课上练习向主人请教做法，说说材料名称和制作步骤，也介绍一样家乡食物。把“请您再示范一次”练熟，准备走进胡同人家，边动手边交流。

After lunch午间至午后

After lunch and a short rest, use one-to-one Chinese tutoring and homework time to prepare for the visit. Rehearse a question for your host and a sentence about your own family’s food traditions, adding detail at your own pace.

午餐、短休后，通过一对一中文辅导完成作业。练练想问主人的问题，也准备一句关于自己家乡味道的介绍，让下午的交流既能请教，也能分享。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Visit a hutong home to learn a traditional craft and make dumplings with your hosts. Share the meal you have helped prepare, then explore Nanluoguxiang’s shops with your teachers, noticing how everyday purchases bring together old traditions and today’s technology.

走进胡同人家，向主人学一门非遗手艺，亲手包饺子、围坐吃晚餐。再随老师逛逛南锣鼓巷，观察传统小店里的扫码点单与支付，发现老街怎样连接今天的生活。

Language Quest今日中文任务

Hutong Life, Made & SharedAsk your host about a craft or a dumpling filling, then describe what you made. In the shops, ask a willing shopkeeper how customers pay and compare the answer with shopping at home.

胡同生活体验官向主人请教一个手艺细节或饺子馅的做法，再介绍自己的作品。逛街时，向愿意交流的店主问问客人怎样付款，听听扫码支付的日常，再和伙伴比较家乡的购物方式。

At the table餐桌风味

Dumplings Made with Our HostsThe dumplings you have helped to make come to the table with Beijing hot dishes and vegetables. Compare everyone’s folds, talk about favourite fillings and listen as your hosts share a little more of their everyday life.

晚餐：和主人亲手包的饺子把亲手包的饺子端上桌，搭配京味热菜与时蔬。看看大家捏出的不同形状，聊聊喜欢的馅，也听主人讲讲家里的日常，在一餐饭里多认识彼此。

DAY 04FRI9JUL

Humanoid Robots & the Language of Movement

人形机器人与未来伙伴养成记

Morning上午

In Chinese class, explore action words and practise explaining a simple sequence. Ask how something works, listen to a partner’s instructions and refine your own, preparing to look closely at the ways a robot learns a movement.

中文课上认识动作词，练习把一个过程说清楚。试着问“它怎样学会这个动作”，再和伙伴轮流说指令、听指令，为下午走进机器人训练现场做好准备。

After lunch午间至午后

After lunch on campus and a short rest, work through homework and practise your questions in one-to-one Chinese tutoring. Your teacher helps you turn curiosity about robots into clear words, from naming a movement to asking why it needs practice.

校内午餐、短休后，在一对一中文辅导中完成作业，练练想向工作人员请教的问题。从一个动作名称到“为什么需要这样训练”，把好奇变成自己说得出的中文。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Visit the humanoid robot data training centre in Shijingshan. Watch how a movement is demonstrated and recorded, try an operation with staff guidance, and discover how small adjustments help a robot work towards an everyday task.

走进石景山人形机器人数据训练中心，看看动作怎样被示范与记录。在工作人员指导下体验操作，观察一次小小调整带来的变化，发现机器人怎样学习完成日常任务。

Language Quest今日中文任务

Today, I Coach a RobotAsk how one movement is taught, then describe what you notice. Use action cards or a short sequence, or explain which part needed adjusting and why; your teacher helps you shape the discovery into your own Chinese.

我给机器人当教练请教一个动作怎样训练，再把观察讲出来。可以借助动作卡和简单步骤，也可以说明哪里需要调整、为什么，让一次科技体验变成自己的中文表达。

At the table餐桌风味

Zhajiangmian & Beijing FavouritesMix savoury zhajiang sauce through noodles with crisp vegetable toppings, then share a selection of Beijing dishes. Compare the combinations you enjoy and tell a friend about a robot movement that surprised you.

晚餐：炸酱面与京味菜拌开咸香炸酱与清爽菜码，尝尝老北京熟悉的一碗面，再分享京味热菜与时蔬。聊聊各自喜欢的搭配，也说说今天哪个机器人动作最让自己惊讶。

DAY 05SAT10JUL

Karting, Smart Rides & the Xiaomi Factory

小米卡丁车与未来出行探索

Morning上午

Begin with the usual three-hour Chinese lesson, exploring words for movement, speed and transport. Practise explaining a preference and asking how a machine works, ready to compare the feeling of a kart with the technology inside a modern car.

上午照常安排3小时中文课，认识运动、速度与交通相关的词句。练习说明喜好、请教机械怎样运转，为下午比较卡丁车和智能汽车准备自己的问题。

After lunch & afternoon午间至午后

After lunch on campus and a short break, travel to Yizhuang for Xiaomi karting. Teachers organise driving turns and observation roles to match age, height and venue rules, so everyone has a part in noticing the steering, braking and teamwork behind the experience.

校内午餐、短休后前往亦庄，体验小米卡丁车。老师根据年龄、身高与现场规则安排驾驶体验和观察任务，大家都能找到参与的角色，留意转向、制动与场上的配合。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

With your teachers, travel in small groups in autonomous vehicles to the Xiaomi car factory for a visit. Compare what you notice during the ride with the manufacturing process, then share a Western-style dinner and your ideas about the transport of the future.

在老师陪同下，分组乘坐无人驾驶汽车前往小米汽车工厂参访，把路上的智能出行体验和眼前的汽车制造联系起来。晚餐换上丰富的西式风味，聊聊自己想象中的未来交通。

Language Quest今日中文任务

My Future Mobility ReviewChoose a moment from karting, the smart ride or the factory visit. Beginners can name an action or a feature; more experienced speakers ask how it works and compare two experiences in a short Chinese review.

未来出行体验官从卡丁车、智能出行或工厂参访中选一个印象深刻的瞬间。初学者说出动作或特点，程度好的孩子请教原理、比较体验，用中文做一段自己的出行测评。

At the table餐桌风味

A Western Table & New IdeasPasta, pizza and warm Western-style dishes offer familiar flavours alongside vegetables and salad. Share the table with your teammates, compare your favourite moments and give the day’s new ideas a little room to grow through conversation.

晚餐：西式风味与伙伴分享意面、披萨与西式热餐，搭配蔬菜和沙拉，让熟悉的味道丰富一周的餐桌。和伙伴分享喜欢的食物，也聊聊卡丁车的体验、工厂的发现与自己的新想法。

DAY 08TUE13JUL

Huanghuacheng: Where the Great Wall Meets Water

黄花城水长城与山间同行

Morning上午

Begin with one-to-one Chinese tutoring and homework time, preparing a few words for the landscape ahead. Leave for Huanghuacheng later that morning, reaching the foot of the mountains for a farmhouse lunch and a comfortable midday break before exploring the Great Wall beside the water.

上午先安排一对一中文辅导与作业时间，准备介绍山水风景的词句，辅导后便提前出发前往黄花城。在山脚下的农家乐吃午餐、稍作休息，养足精神再去看水长城。

Afternoon下午

Take a boat across the water and discover the Great Wall following the mountain ridges. Walk with your teachers at a comfortable group pace, pause in the shade and look closely at how stone walls, green hills and water frame one another.

乘船看长城沿山势起伏，再随老师沿步道徒步，按团队节奏边走边看。找一处树荫歇歇脚，观察石墙、青山与水面的关系，把眼前的景色讲给伙伴听。

Evening晚间

Return to camp for a shower and a change of clothes before a warm northern-style dinner. With the day’s photographs and notes nearby, share a favourite view or a small moment of encouragement from the walk, then settle in for a restful evening.

返营后先洗澡、换上干爽衣服，再享用温热的北方风味晚餐。翻翻今天的照片与笔记，分享喜欢的风景或路上互相鼓励的小片段，轻松收尾，好好休息。
