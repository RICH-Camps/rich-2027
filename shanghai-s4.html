The RICH Summer

Find inspiration in Shanghai. Make it your own in Jingdezhen.

把上海的灵感，画进自己的青花故事

A window along Wukang Road, a woven pattern or the curve of a leaf can become the first mark in a sketchbook. Ask how a car is designed and how a ceramic shape is made, then take your ideas to Jingdezhen. With artisans and teachers beside them, children shape clay, paint blue-and-white patterns and find the Chinese words to explain a choice that is entirely their own.

武康路的一扇窗、土布上的一组配色、温室里的一片叶子，都可以成为创作的开始。孩子带着问题走进博物馆、工坊与汽车科技现场，再把在上海积累的灵感带到景德镇。师傅示范，老师陪着提问；从泥土在手中成形，到青花落在备好的瓷坯上，把自己的灵感变成作品，也用中文讲清创作背后的想法。

The RICH Method · 学过的中文，带到生活里

Before every outing, teachers demonstrate and rehearse a language quest, then guide students at their own level. Beginners start with observation and prompt cards; more experienced speakers follow up, compare and explain. One-to-one Chinese tutoring—a RICH commitment for over a decade—gives each child time to practise with a teacher and make an expression their own.

每次外出都有中文任务。老师先示范、陪练，再按水平带队实践：初学者从观察和词句卡起步，已有基础的孩子尝试追问、比较和说明理由。坚持十余年的一对一中文辅导，让每个孩子都有和老师细细打磨表达的时间。

A question asked with a little more courage can become a conversation, and a conversation can become a friendship. Through a handmade piece, a shared meal or a story told in their own words, children find a sense of belonging in another culture—and moments of beauty to enjoy together, whatever language they grew up speaking.

勇敢问出的一个问题，可能成为一段对话、一份友谊。一件亲手做的作品、一张围坐的餐桌，让陌生的文化渐渐有了亲切感。孩子把所见所感写进笔记、讲成故事，也发现：即使说着不同的母语，总有值得一起欣赏的美。

Around our table · 尝新，也有熟悉的味道

Learn to make xiaolongbao with the chef, explore Jiading’s local flavours and choose dinner alongside university students. Japanese, Korean and Western meals add familiar favourites to Shanghai’s traditional table. In Jingdezhen, discover jiaozi ba rice dumplings, jianshui ba rice cakes and home-style Jiangxi dishes, asking about the ingredients and sharing the day’s creations over dinner.

跟师傅学做小笼包，尝尝嘉定地方风味，也和大学生一起去食堂选餐。日餐、韩餐与西餐穿插其间，让孩子吃得丰富，也有熟悉的味道。到了景德镇，再从饺子粑、碱水粑与赣味家常菜认识瓷都生活，问问食材、交换口味，也聊聊今天手里慢慢成形的作品。

Menus, seasoning and meal order are thoughtfully adapted to the day’s activities, students’ tastes and dietary needs.

具体菜品、辣度与用餐顺序，会结合当天活动、孩子的口味与饮食需求灵活安排。

DAY 01THU22JUL

Hello Shanghai, Hello New Friends

华师大迎新，从一声你好开始

Arrivals & settling in抵达与安顿

The RICH team welcomes you at Shanghai airport and accompanies you to camp at East China Normal University. Unpack, freshen up and take time to rest. Teachers help everyone settle into the campus and get to know a first new friend.

老师在上海机场迎接大家，陪同前往华东师范大学营地。安顿行李、洗漱休整，慢慢熟悉校园，也记住第一位新伙伴的名字，让远道而来的第一天从安心开始。

Evening晚间

Share a Shanghai welcome dinner, exchange hometown stories and discover a common interest across the table. Hear what the coming days hold, from the shapes of city buildings to clay taking shape in your own hands, and talk about what you hope to create.

欢迎晚宴上，交换家乡与兴趣，找找同桌伙伴和自己的共同点。从上海街头的建筑线条，到将在手中慢慢成形的一团泥，聊聊这趟旅程最期待的发现，也听听新朋友最想尝试什么。

欢迎晚宴：本帮菜红烧肉、八宝鸭与时蔬，带来本帮菜丰盛而细致的欢迎。尝尝熟悉或新鲜的味道，记住身边伙伴的名字，让一桌热菜成为新友谊的开场。

校内午餐、短休后，和老师进行一对一中文辅导，完成作业。把想问的问题先说一遍，练习几个动作词，让每个孩子都能带着适合自己的表达走进下午的活动。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Find your way around ECNU with teachers and new friends, then learn kung fu movements together. Watch, listen and try a short sequence, using Chinese to help a partner remember a movement. Campus conversations continue over dinner alongside university students.

跟着老师和新朋友认识华师大校园，再一起学习中国功夫。看动作、听口令、试着把一小段招式连起来，用中文提醒伙伴一个动作；晚餐时和大学生走进食堂，继续聊聊校园生活。

At the table餐桌风味

Dinner with University StudentsJoin university students in the campus dining hall, choose a meal from the counters and find a place together. A favourite dish can start a conversation about classes, clubs and the everyday rhythm of university life.

晚餐：走进大学食堂和大学生一起到食堂选餐、打饭、找座位。从今天喜欢的一道菜聊起，听听他们的课程、社团与日常，感受一顿饭里自然发生的大学生活。

DAY 03SAT24JUL

Wukang Road, Anfu Road & an Eye for Detail

武康路与安福路，收藏梧桐光影

Morning上午

In a three-hour Chinese lesson, explore words for colour, shape and the small details that catch your eye. Practise saying what you notice and why you like it, turning a simple description into a point of view you can share.

上午3小时中文课，学习描述颜色、形状和让自己停下脚步的细节。从“我看到了什么”到“我为什么喜欢”，把一次观察说清楚，也慢慢形成自己的审美表达。

After lunch午间至午后

After lunch and a short rest, use one-to-one Chinese tutoring and homework time to prepare your own observation phrases. Choose words you can use confidently, then try adding a comparison or a reason before heading into the neighbourhood with teachers.

午餐、短休后，进行一对一中文辅导并完成作业。和老师准备适合自己的观察用语，先把看见的细节说准确，再试着多加一个比较或理由，带着自己的视角出发。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Walk beneath the plane trees around Wukang Road and Anfu Road, noticing curved balconies, doorways and the way old buildings meet creative shopfronts. Pause to photograph a detail, compare it with a friend’s choice and ask about something that catches your curiosity.

沿着武康路、安福路的梧桐街区慢慢走，看看阳台的弧线、门窗的轮廓，以及老建筑旁有趣的小店。停下来拍一处心动细节，和伙伴交换视角，也把自己的好奇变成一个问题，试着与人聊一聊。

My Shanghai Light & Shadow CollectionChoose three details that make the neighbourhood feel like Shanghai and add a Chinese caption to each photograph. Use a colour or shape word to begin, or explain how one detail changes the mood of a street. Share your favourite with the group.

梧桐光影收藏家拍下三处让你感到“这很上海”的细节，为照片配上中文说明。可以从颜色、形状起步，也可以说说一个细节怎样改变了街道的气质，再向伙伴介绍自己的最爱。

At the table餐桌风味

Scallion Noodles & a Shanghai SweetScallion-oil noodles come with warm dishes and vegetables after an afternoon on foot. A crisp butterfly pastry adds a little sweetness to the day’s collection of Shanghai details.

晚餐：葱油拌面与热菜一碗葱油拌面搭配热菜与时蔬，为街区漫步添上踏实的滋味。再尝一口酥脆的蝴蝶酥，把上海的甜香也收进今天的记忆。

DAY 04SUN25JUL

Shanghai Museum East & the Beauty of Objects

上博东馆，发现器物里的中国审美

Morning上午

During a three-hour Chinese lesson, learn to describe materials, patterns and the possible uses of an object. Practise introducing one favourite item, then asking a thoughtful question about the choices behind its shape or decoration.

上午3小时中文课，学习介绍一件器物的材质、纹样与用途。选一个自己喜欢的物品说一说，再练习追问它为什么这样设计，为下午走进博物馆准备自己的问题。

After lunch午间至午后

After lunch and a short rest, meet your teacher for one-to-one Chinese tutoring and homework time. Work with a picture or a short description, building the words and sentences that will help you explain what makes an object special.

午餐、短休后，进行一对一中文辅导，完成作业。借助图片或一小段介绍，和老师打磨自己的观察用语，让一个喜欢的器物细节也能被别人听懂。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Explore Shanghai Museum East, looking closely at ceramics and Jiangnan craftsmanship. Notice how a vessel’s curve, a painted motif or a small handle can change the whole design. Collect sketches and observations that may find a new life in your own pottery later in the journey.

走进上海博物馆东馆，细看陶瓷与江南工艺里的线条和纹样。一道弧线、一处图案、一个小小的把手，怎样改变了一件器物的样子？把观察画下来、记下来，为之后的陶艺创作积累灵感。

Language Quest今日中文任务

The Pattern Inspiration ArchiveChoose one object and collect a material word, a pattern detail and a question about its design. Beginners can build an illustrated word card; more experienced speakers can explain which detail they might reinterpret in their own ceramic work.

文物纹样灵感库选一件器物，记录一种材质、一处纹样和一个设计问题。初学者可以制作图文词卡，已有基础的孩子试着说明：将来做自己的陶瓷时，最想借鉴其中哪一个细节。

At the table餐桌风味

Korean Barbecue & a Warming Noodle PotKorean grilled beef, vegetables and noodles in a shared budae-jjigae pot bring a change of flavour to the evening. Around the table, compare the museum details each person chose and discover how differently friends can see the same object.

晚餐：韩式烤牛肉与部队锅韩式烤牛肉配上蔬菜，再分享热气腾腾的部队锅煮面，为晚餐换一种滋味。边吃边聊各自在博物馆的发现，听听同一件器物在伙伴眼里有怎样不同的美。

DAY 05MON26JUL

Yuyuan Old Town & a Xiaolongbao Masterclass

豫园老城厢与小笼包大师课

Morning上午

In a three-hour Chinese lesson, practise asking about ingredients, describing texture and explaining a short sequence of actions. Listen for the words a chef might use, then rehearse how to ask for help or describe a small discovery of your own.

上午3小时中文课，练习询问食材、比较口感和说明制作步骤。听一听师傅可能用到的词语，也练习怎样开口请教，为下午一边动手、一边交流做好准备。

After lunch午间至午后

After a campus lunch and a short rest, enjoy one-to-one Chinese tutoring and homework time. Practise your own questions about folding, filling and flavour, with time to repeat a useful phrase until it feels comfortable to say.

校内午餐、短休后，进行一对一中文辅导并完成作业。围绕包法、馅料和口味准备自己的问题，把一句实用表达再练熟一点，下午就能更从容地和师傅交流。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Explore Yuyuan’s old-town streets before joining a xiaolongbao masterclass. The chef teaches the craft personally and patiently, demonstrating delicate pleats and helping each learner try again. Feel how a thin wrapper becomes a little parcel of broth, and ask about the detail you find hardest.

走进豫园老城厢，再跟师傅上一堂小笼包大师课。师傅亲自示范、耐心带教，一点点陪孩子捏出褶子、调整手势，在反复尝试中体会薄皮包住汤汁的门道，也把最想请教的问题说出口。

舌尖上的造物师一边动手，一边用中文说出一个步骤，再给自己的小笼包做个小介绍。可以从食材和动作词起步，也可以讲讲哪一步没做好、师傅怎样提醒，以及再试一次有什么不同。

At the table餐桌风味

上午3小时中文课，认识手作工具与动作词，练习请师傅示范一个步骤。再试着介绍一组配色，说说自己希望作品带给别人怎样的感觉，让创作从一个清楚的想法开始。

DAY 08THU29JUL
