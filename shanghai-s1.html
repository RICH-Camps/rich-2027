The RICH Summer

From Shanghai discoveries to new friendships in Beijing.

从黄浦江到未名湖，带着自己的故事认识新朋友

Learn a delicate fold from a Shanghai dumpling chef, cheer for a university friend on the playing field and give the riverside skyline a Chinese voice of your own. Then take your stories to Beijing, where new campmates are ready to share their city. Teachers help each child join the conversation at their own level, until unfamiliar streets have familiar faces and a first greeting has become a friendship.

在小笼包师傅身边学一个细细的捏褶动作，在华师大操场用中文为新朋友加油，再给黄浦江的灯火配上自己的旁白。带着这些上海故事来到北京，又有一群伙伴等着一起逛校园、演短剧、走进电影世界。老师陪孩子从适合自己的表达开始，让两座城市渐渐有了熟悉的面孔，也让一句问候有了继续聊下去的可能。

The RICH Method · 学过的中文，带到生活里

Before every outing, teachers demonstrate and rehearse a language quest, then guide students at their own level. Beginners start with observation and prompt cards; more experienced speakers follow up, compare and explain. One-to-one Chinese tutoring—a RICH commitment for over a decade—gives each child time to practise with a teacher and make an expression their own.

每次外出都有中文任务。老师先示范、陪练，再按水平带队实践：初学者从观察和词句卡起步，已有基础的孩子尝试追问、比较和说明理由。坚持十余年的一对一中文辅导，让每个孩子都有和老师细细打磨表达的时间。

A question asked with a little more courage can become a conversation, and a conversation can become a friendship. Through a handmade piece, a shared meal or a story told in their own words, children find a sense of belonging in another culture—and moments of beauty to enjoy together, whatever language they grew up speaking.

勇敢问出的一个问题，可能成为一段对话、一份友谊。一件亲手做的作品、一张围坐的餐桌，让陌生的文化渐渐有了亲切感。孩子把所见所感写进笔记、讲成故事，也发现：即使说着不同的母语，总有值得一起欣赏的美。

Around our table · 尝新，也有熟悉的味道

Learn the craft of xiaolongbao from a chef, choose a favourite plate of conveyor-belt sushi and share Peking duck with new friends in Beijing. Korean grilled beef and a Western farewell table bring variety along the way. At ECNU and PKU, dinner beside university students lets conversations continue beyond the campus walk, with a place at the table for familiar flavours and new discoveries alike.

跟师傅学小笼包的手艺，在旋转寿司台前自己挑选喜欢的口味，再到北京和新伙伴一起卷烤鸭薄饼。韩式烤牛肉与西式欢送餐穿插其间，尝新也有熟悉的滋味。在华师大与北大，和大学生一起去食堂选餐，把校园里的话题接到餐桌边，慢慢认识彼此的生活。

Menus, seasoning and meal order are thoughtfully adapted to the day’s activities, students’ tastes and dietary needs.

具体菜品、辣度与用餐顺序，会结合当天活动、孩子的口味与饮食需求灵活安排。

A Shanghai Welcome & Your New Camp Home

上海接机与华师大营地迎新

All day全天

The RICH team welcomes you at Shanghai airport and helps you settle into the ECNU camp. Unpack, rest after the journey and get to know the teachers who will share these two cities with you.

老师在上海机场迎接大家，陪同前往华师大营地报到。安顿行李、休息一下，认识接下来一路陪伴的老师，让双城旅程从安心的第一天开始。

Evening晚间

Gather over a Shanghai welcome dinner and join Chinese icebreaker games with your new campmates. Swap names, hometowns and favourite things; the people around the table will soon be sharing classes, discoveries and stories with you.

欢迎晚宴上，和新伙伴交换名字、聊聊家乡与爱好，再一起参加中文破冰游戏。今天餐桌旁还不太熟悉的面孔，接下来会和你一起上课、探索、分享故事。

Language Quest今日中文任务

My First Camp ConversationIntroduce yourself with a name and an interest, with a teacher or a prompt card nearby if you need one. Find something in common and invite a new friend to keep talking.

我的中文搭子上线从名字和一个爱好说起，需要时让老师或词句卡帮一把。在破冰游戏里找到一个共同话题，试着邀请新朋友接着聊。

At the table餐桌风味

A Shanghai Welcome DinnerA welcoming table of Shanghai dishes brings red-braised pork, traditional specialities such as eight-treasure duck, and seasonal vegetables together. Share dishes and stories as teachers and new campmates help everyone feel at home.

欢迎晚宴：本帮菜红烧肉、八宝鸭等本帮风味，搭配时蔬与温热主食，为远道而来的孩子接风。老师和伙伴围坐分享，在轻松的第一餐里聊家乡、记名字，慢慢找到营地生活的亲切感。

DAY 02SUN13JUN

ECNU Campus, Kung Fu & New Friends

华师大校园功夫与新伙伴

Morning上午

A Chinese assessment helps teachers place you in a class suited to your level. During the first three-hour lesson, practise a friendly introduction, a question about campus and the simple instructions that help people learn and play together.

通过中文水平测试，进入适合自己的班级。第一堂3小时中文课里，练习介绍自己、请教校园方向，也认识几个动作口令，为下午和大学生交流、学功夫做好准备。

After lunch午间至午后

After campus lunch and a short rest, work on homework during one-to-one Chinese tutoring. Rehearse a question for a university student and practise saying a movement clearly, with your teacher helping you find the words you need.

校内午餐、短休后，在一对一中文辅导中完成作业。练练想问大学生的问题，再试着说清一个动作，让老师陪你把下午要用的中文先说熟。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Explore the ECNU campus and chat with university students about their everyday lives. Learn a few kung fu movements, then join games with your campmates: follow a Chinese instruction, try a move together and cheer on someone you have just met.

探索华师大校园，和大学生聊学习与日常生活。学几招中国功夫，再和伙伴一起运动：听一个中文口令、练一个动作，也为刚认识的朋友加油，让校园里的相识多一些笑声与默契。

Language Quest今日中文任务

My Campus Kung Fu PartnerUse a question prepared with your teacher to discover a campus spot. Then teach a partner one kung fu movement in Chinese, beginning with an action word or explaining the whole sequence.

校园隐藏地图与功夫搭子带着老师一起练过的问题，发现一处校园角落；再用中文教伙伴一招功夫。从一个动作词到一段完整说明，把刚学会的本领分享出去。

At the table餐桌风味

Dinner with ECNU StudentsAfter the afternoon’s games, head to a campus dining hall with ECNU students. Browse the food counters, choose your dinner and carry conversations from the playing field to the table—sharing interests and getting to know their everyday campus life.

晚餐：和华师大学生一起吃食堂运动过后，和华师大学生一起去食堂，看看各个窗口的选择，挑一份自己喜欢的晚餐。把操场上的话题接到餐桌边，聊聊兴趣，也认识他们平常的校园生活。

DAY 03MON14JUN

Yuyuan & a Xiaolongbao Masterclass

豫园老城厢与小笼包大师课

Morning上午

Learn how to ask about ingredients, describe flavours and request a cooking tip in Chinese. Practise putting a few steps in order, ready to learn from a dumpling chef in the afternoon.

中文课上练习问食材、说口味，也试着向师傅请教做法。用“先、再、最后”把几个步骤连起来，为下午的小笼包体验备好词句。

After lunch午间至午后

After lunch and a short rest, work on homework and rehearse a question for the chef during one-to-one Chinese tutoring. Begin with an ingredient name or ask about the craft behind a soup dumpling.

午餐、短休后，在一对一中文辅导中完成作业，练练想问师傅的问题。从一个食材名称到一句有关手艺的追问，老师陪你找到合适的开口方式。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

In Yuyuan’s old streets, join a xiaolongbao masterclass led personally by the chef. Watch each fold, then shape your own dumplings with patient, hands-on guidance. There is time to ask, try again and enjoy the delight of making something together.

走进豫园老城厢，上一堂由小笼包师傅亲自带教的大师课。看师傅示范捏褶，再自己动手；哪里没掌握，师傅就耐心拆解、陪着再试，让每个孩子都享受学手艺的乐趣。

Language Quest今日中文任务

The Craft Inside a Soup DumplingAsk the chef a question prepared in class and try a step yourself. Name an ingredient or explain the process using “first, then, finally” to a friend.

小笼包非遗解码局带着课堂准备的问题向师傅请教，试做一个步骤；从食材名称到“先、再、最后”，把学到的手艺说给伙伴听。

At the table餐桌风味

Nanxiang Soup DumplingsTaste the thin wrapper and savoury broth of Nanxiang-style soup dumplings, alongside warm dishes, clear soup and vegetables for a varied Shanghai meal.

晚餐：南翔小笼尝尝南翔小笼的薄皮与鲜汤，边吃边回想刚学过的做法，再搭配热菜、清汤与蔬菜，认识上海的手艺滋味。

DAY 04TUE15JUN

Bailian ZX & Shared Fandoms

百联ZX与青年文化

Morning上午

Compare characters, styles and designs in Chinese class. Practise saying what you like and why, then try asking a classmate about a different choice and listening for something you had not noticed.

中文课上比较角色与设计，练习把“我喜欢”说得更具体。听听同学为什么选择另一个角色，试着问一句，也发现自己没注意过的细节。

After lunch午间至午后

Enjoy campus lunch and a quiet midday break. Look over a character or design you would like to talk about, and bring one question that could open a conversation with a fellow fan.

校内午餐后安排午间休整。看看自己想聊的角色与设计，准备一句可以向同好提问的话，带着共同的兴趣轻松出发。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Step into Bailian ZX's world of anime, collectibles and character design. Begin a conversation through a shared favourite and hear what interests young people in China, including the choices that differ from your own.

走进百联ZX，逛逛潮玩与动漫空间，从共同喜欢的角色聊起。听听中国年轻人的兴趣与想法，发现相同趣味，也认识不一样的选择。

Language Quest今日中文任务

Find Your Fellow FanAsk someone about a favourite character and share your own choice. Listen for the reason behind theirs, and find a detail that gives you more to talk about.

潮玩同好接头计划聊聊最喜欢的角色，问问对方为什么喜欢。试着说清自己的选择，也从对方的回答里找到一个可以继续聊的细节。

At the table餐桌风味

Conveyor-Belt SushiAfter exploring Bailian ZX, enjoy the playful rhythm of conveyor-belt sushi. Watch the colourful plates pass by, choose the ones you like and compare discoveries with friends—dinner becomes a little adventure in its own right.

晚餐：旋转寿司，自选自己的好味道百联ZX探索之后，围坐旋转寿司台前，看一盘盘色彩丰富的寿司从眼前经过，自由挑选喜欢的口味。和伙伴交换推荐，让晚餐也多一点自己做主的乐趣。

DAY 05WED16JUN

Suzhou Creek Stories & a Huangpu Night Cruise

苏州河城市漫步与浦江夜游

Morning上午

Practise describing a view and comparing old and new in Chinese. Choose words for a detail that catches your eye, and prepare a few lines of commentary for the city scenes you will encounter.

中文课上练习描述风景、比较今昔。可以从“我看到”说起，也可以试着解释一个喜欢的细节，为下午的城市镜头准备中文解说。

After lunch午间至午后

Take a midday break after lunch before setting off along the river. Bring a question about the city, a few useful words and time to look closely at what others might walk past.

午餐后安排午间休整，再带着观察问题出发。想想自己最想了解的城市细节，也准备几个词，留心那些平时可能匆匆走过的风景。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Follow Suzhou Creek past Sihang Warehouse, Waibaidu Bridge and the Broadway Mansions. After dinner, board a Huangpu River cruise and watch the lights unfold on both banks, comparing the city's older streets with its modern skyline.

沿苏州河看四行仓库、外白渡桥与上海大厦，读一读城市留下的故事。晚餐后乘船夜游黄浦江，看两岸灯火铺开，让老街与新天际线相遇。

Language Quest今日中文任务

Shanghai, Then & NowChoose an old building and a view of the modern skyline. Start with what you can see, then compare the two and add your own Chinese commentary.

魔都时空剪辑师选一处老建筑和一段新天际线，从“我看到……”说起；试着比较两种城市风景，为喜欢的画面配一句中文解说。

At the table餐桌风味

Shanghai Vegetarian FlavoursA table of mushrooms, tofu and seasonal vegetables reveals another side of Shanghai cooking, with varied textures and warm staple dishes before the evening cruise.

晚餐：海派素食用菌菇、豆制品与时蔬认识海派素食的不同口感，搭配温热主食，在夜游浦江前，享受一餐清爽而丰富的滋味。

上海迪士尼与童话里的新朋友

Park meals园区餐食

Meals in the MagicTheme-park dining brings a playful setting to the day's meals, with warm food and time to recharge before the next adventure together.

Beijing 北京

DAYS 09–16 · JUN 20–27

Bring your Shanghai discoveries to Beijing, and find new friends to share the next chapter.

带着上海的发现走进北京，和新伙伴一起，把双城故事接着写下去。

DAY 09SUN20JUN

Hello Beijing, Hello New Friends

抵达北京，与新伙伴汇合

Morning上午

Arrive in Beijing after the overnight journey and travel to the residence with the team. Shower, unpack, enjoy breakfast and take time to rest before the Beijing part of camp begins.

夜卧抵达北京，随老师前往驻地。洗澡、安顿行李，吃好早餐后充分休息，让身体慢慢从旅途转入北京的营地生活。

After lunch & afternoon午间至午后

After lunch and a short rest, a full three-hour Chinese lesson continues the learning at your existing level. Revisit the language you used in Shanghai and prepare to introduce yourself to new RICH campmates in Beijing.

午餐、短休后，下午保留完整3小时中文课，按已有水平衔接学习。回顾在上海用过的表达，也准备好向北京的新伙伴介绍自己。

Evening晚间

Meet the Beijing campers over a warm welcome dinner. Introduce someone from your Shanghai group, share a story from the past week and discover a common interest with a new friend who will join you for the days ahead.

和北京营地的新伙伴共进欢迎晚宴，也把上海一路同行的朋友介绍给大家。分享过去一周的趣事，听听新朋友的家乡与爱好，让接下来一起上课、探索的日子，从一桌热闹的相识开始。

Language Quest今日中文任务

Shanghai Stories, New Friends in BeijingShare a Shanghai story and ask about a new friend’s hometown or interests. Find a Beijing experience you are both looking forward to, and bring each other into the conversation.

上海故事，北京新朋友分享一个上海故事，听听新朋友的家乡和爱好。找到一件共同期待的北京体验，也主动把身边的伙伴带进话题，让新的朋友圈慢慢展开。

At the table餐桌风味

A Warm Beijing WelcomeA generous table of Beijing home-style dishes, vegetables and warm rice or noodles welcomes everyone after the journey. Sit with teachers and new campmates, swap stories from home and learn one another’s names over a relaxed first dinner full of conversation and laughter.

欢迎晚宴：京味家常菜京酱肉丝等家常热菜，配上清爽时蔬与温热主食，为远道而来的孩子接风。老师和伙伴围坐聊家乡、说趣事，在笑声里记住彼此的名字，用轻松热情的第一餐开启北京生活。

DAY 10MON21JUN

PKU Campus & My University Friends

北大校园与我的大学朋友

Morning上午

Continue your Chinese learning alongside your new Beijing classmates during a three-hour lesson. Draw on your time at ECNU to prepare questions about PKU: what is a student’s day like, and which place on campus matters most to them?

3小时中文课里，和北京的新同学一起接着原有进度学习。想想在华师大的发现，再准备想问北大学生的问题：他们一天怎样度过，最喜欢校园里的哪一处？

After lunch午间至午后

After lunch and a short rest, settle into one-to-one Chinese tutoring and homework time. Practise an opening question, work through unfamiliar words and prepare for conversations around campus.

午餐、短休后，和老师进行一对一中文辅导，完成作业。把开场白和想问的问题说一遍，练熟词句，带着准备走进校园。

Afternoon下午

Walk through PKU, visiting its museum and campus gift shop along the way. Hear stories of the university, chat with students about study and everyday life, and find a campus corner that speaks to you.

漫步北大，逛逛校内博物馆与文创店，听校园里的历史故事。再和大学生聊学习与生活，找一处自己喜欢的角落，说说喜欢它的理由。

Evening晚间

After dinner, join a swimming group or choose basketball, football, volleyball or badminton. With teachers organising the groups, there is time to move, cheer one another on and get to know your campmates.

晚餐后，由老师组织分组，选择游泳，或参加篮球、足球、排球、羽毛球。在传球、配合和加油声中，让新伙伴渐渐熟悉起来。

Language Quest今日中文任务

Two Campuses, New PerspectivesAsk a PKU student about a favourite campus spot or part of university life. Use a prompt card to begin, or follow up by comparing it with something you discovered at ECNU; introduce one new university friend to your group.

我的双城大学朋友圈问问北大学生喜欢哪处校园风景，或最享受怎样的大学生活。可以借助词句卡开口，也可以把华师大的发现带进交流，比较两所大学的日常，再向伙伴介绍一位新认识的大学朋友。

At the table餐桌风味

Dinner with PKU StudentsJoin PKU students in a campus dining hall, choose dishes from different counters and ask for their favourites. Over dinner, chat about classes, clubs and the everyday moments that make university life their own.

晚餐：和北大学子一起吃食堂和北大学子一起去学校食堂，从不同窗口挑选喜欢的菜品，也请他们推荐平常爱吃的味道。坐下来边吃边聊课程、社团与校园趣事，走近大学生日常。

DAY 11TUE22JUN

Hutong Discoveries & Peking Duck

南锣胡同与北京烤鸭

Morning上午

In Chinese class, turn your curiosity about handmade objects into questions. Practise asking about materials and methods, and listen to a partner’s explanation before trying a question of your own.

中文课上，把对手工作品的好奇变成问题。练习问材料、请教做法，和伙伴轮流介绍一样物品，再试着顺着对方的回答继续聊。

After lunch午间至午后

After lunch and a short rest, work on homework and rehearse your craft-shop conversation during one-to-one Chinese tutoring. Practise an opening question and a phrase that helps you ask for a closer look or a demonstration.

午餐、短休后，通过一对一中文辅导完成作业，练习下午和店主交流的词句。说熟一句开场白，再试着请对方展示做法，带着准备去发现胡同里的手艺。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Wander through Nanluoguxiang with your teachers, looking closely at the craft shops tucked into the hutong lanes. Ask a shopkeeper about something that catches your eye, then gather over Peking duck to share the stories you have discovered.

跟老师走进南锣鼓巷，逛逛胡同里的传统手艺小店。找到一件好奇的物品，向店主请教它的故事；晚餐一起卷烤鸭薄饼，把下午的新发现聊给伙伴听。

Language Quest今日中文任务

The Hutong Discovery NotebookChoose an object in a craft shop and ask what it is made from. Start with a word or a simple question, then follow the maker’s answer to discover a detail worth sharing with your group.

胡同生活发现局在手艺小店里选一件好奇的物品，问问它用什么做成。从一个词、一句提问开始，再顺着店主的回答追问一个细节，把新发现带回小组。

At the table餐桌风味

Peking Duck & Beijing FlavoursDiscover the layers of a duck pancake: crisp skin, soft wrapping and fresh accompaniments, with vegetables and other dishes to share around the table.

晚餐：北京烤鸭从薄饼、鸭肉到葱丝与甜面酱，尝尝一卷烤鸭里的丰富层次，搭配时蔬和热菜，分享一桌北京味道。

DAY 12WED23JUN

A Wuxia Story & Beijing After Dark

中轴线武侠短剧与鸟巢夜色

Morning上午

Choose a character and build a short wuxia adventure with your classmates. Teachers help you shape the dialogue, from one memorable line to a lively exchange, ready for an afternoon performance.

中文课上和伙伴选角色、商量武侠故事，再请老师帮忙打磨台词。可以从一句有趣的话开始，也可以试着把角色之间的对话接下去。

After lunch午间至午后

Lunch and a quiet midday break leave time to recharge before heading out. Look over your lines with a partner and bring your character, imagination and curiosity along for the afternoon.

午餐后留出午间休整，把精神养足。出发前和伙伴看看台词，想想自己的角色会怎样说话，带着故事和好奇心去见北京的中轴线。

Afternoon下午

Climb Jingshan for a view over the Forbidden City and Beijing's Central Axis. Dress in period costumes and perform your short Chinese wuxia scene with friends, turning the morning's words into a shared story.

登上景山，俯瞰故宫与中轴线铺展的城市景象。换上古装，和伙伴用中文演一段武侠短剧，让上午商量的台词走进眼前的江湖。

Evening晚间

Visit the Bird's Nest plaza as Beijing settles into its summer evening. Join local square dancers, ask an older resident to show you a move and enjoy the music, laughter and company.

夜游鸟巢广场，听音乐响起，看北京人的夏夜慢慢热闹起来。向跳广场舞的大爷大妈请教一个动作，跟着节拍试一试，也聊一聊。

Language Quest今日中文任务

A Story Along the Central AxisGive your character a line of your own. Later, ask a square-dance partner to teach you a move, and try following a Chinese instruction.

中轴线穿越剧场给角色一句自己的台词；再向广场舞伙伴请教一个动作，听懂一句口令，把中文用在故事里，也用在新认识的人面前。

At the table餐桌风味

Zhajiangmian & Beijing FavouritesExplore the contrast of savoury sauce and fresh vegetable toppings in Beijing noodles, alongside local dishes and a little sweetness from traditional milk desserts.

晚餐：炸酱面与京味菜拌开炸酱与清爽菜码，认识老北京熟悉的一碗面；京味热菜与宫廷奶酪，让咸香和甜润在餐桌上相遇。

DAY 13THU24JUN

Universal Beijing & Pop-Culture Conversations

北京环球影城与流行文化小记者

All day全天

Spend a joyful day at Universal Beijing in teacher-led groups, stepping into favourite movie worlds with friends. Then take a brave step beyond your own group: meet Chinese Gen-Z park-goers and discover the characters, stories and imagined worlds you love in common.

老师分组带队畅玩北京环球影城，和伙伴走进喜爱的电影世界。再勇敢迈出熟悉的小圈子，与中国Z世代游客互动，从角色、故事和共同喜爱的电影世界聊起。

Meals & return to camp用餐与返营

Take breaks for lunch and dinner inside the park, then return to camp for a rest. Share a favourite moment or an unexpected interview answer with your friends as the day winds down.

在园区安排午晚餐，游玩间隙坐下来补充体力，结束后返营休息。和伙伴回味最开心的片段，也分享采访里听到的有趣回答。

Language Quest今日中文任务

The Pop-Culture ReporterRehearse your reporter’s opening, then invite a willing Chinese Gen-Z visitor to a short interview in Chinese. Ask which movie world they would step into and why; ask a follow-up question about an interesting answer, and bring a new perspective back to your group.

流行文化小记者练好小记者的开场白，征得同意后，用中文街访中国Z世代游客：最想走进哪个电影世界，为什么？顺着一个有趣的回答继续聊，把不同的想法带回小组。

Park meals园区餐食

Dining in the Movie WorldsEnjoy meals in the park’s movie-inspired settings, with time to sit down, recharge and swap the most surprising or entertaining answers from your interviews.

午晚餐：园区美食在电影主题的餐饮空间里坐下来，吃好午晚餐、补充体力。和伙伴交换街访中最有趣的回答，让陌生人的故事也成为今天的精彩片段。

DAY 14FRI25JUN

Humanoid Robots & Future Companions

人形机器人与未来伙伴

Morning上午

Explore the Chinese words for actions and practise describing a simple process. Try asking how something works and why, preparing to look closely at the movements of a new kind of learning partner.

中文课上认识动作词，练习把一个过程说清楚。从“它在做什么”到“为什么这样做”，为下午观察机器人的学习过程准备问题。

After lunch午间至午后

After lunch and a short rest, use one-to-one Chinese tutoring and homework time to explore the language of movement. Your teacher helps turn your curiosity about robots into questions you feel ready to ask.

午餐、短休后，在一对一中文辅导中完成作业，练习描述动作的词句。和老师把对机器人的好奇整理成问题，找到适合自己程度的表达。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Visit the humanoid robot data training centre in Shijingshan for a hands-on introduction. Watch how a small movement is taught, try an operation and ask the staff about the learning process behind it.

走进石景山人形机器人数据训练中心，观察并体验操作。从一个小动作开始，向工作人员请教机器人怎样练习，发现动作背后的训练过程。

Language Quest今日中文任务

Today, I Coach a RobotPractise action words with your teacher, then ask how a robot learns one movement. Share what you notice through a word, a sentence or a fuller explanation.

我给机器人当教练跟老师练练动作词，向工作人员请教一个动作怎样训练；用一个词、一句话或一段说明，把观察讲给伙伴听。

At the table餐桌风味

Korean Beef Barbecue & Noodle HotpotGather around Korean grilled beef and a bubbling budae-jjigae hotpot with noodles. Pass dishes, swap stories and enjoy a warm, lively dinner with friends after an afternoon of new discoveries.

晚餐：韩式烤牛肉与部队锅面韩式烤牛肉的香气，配上部队锅里咕嘟煮开的面条。大家围桌分享，在热气腾腾的晚餐里，聊聊机器人的新发现和一天的趣事。

DAY 15SAT26JUN

798 Art & a Two-City Farewell

798艺术创作与双城欢送晚宴

Morning上午

Look back over both cities during a three-hour Chinese class, with the final hour set aside for the end-of-camp assessment. Revisit your notes and creations, and choose a word or idea from the journey to carry into your final afternoon artwork.

上午3小时中文课回顾双城所学，最后1小时在课内完成结业测评。翻翻学习手册与作品，从旅程中选一个喜欢的词或想法，为下午的创作留下自己的表达。

After lunch午间至午后

Enjoy campus lunch and a restful break before the final afternoon outing. Sketch an idea for your artwork and choose a memory you would like to share with the friends who have become part of your two-city journey.

校内午餐后短休，为最后一次外出与欢送晚宴养足精神。给作品画一张小草图，也选一个想讲给朋友听的片段，看看哪些相遇已经成为自己的双城记忆。

Afternoon & evening下午至晚间

Explore the creative spaces inside 798’s former factories, then turn a Chinese word or idea into your own graffiti artwork. At the farewell dinner with your new friends in Beijing, share your creation, remember a moment you experienced together and thank someone who helped you feel at home.

走进798旧厂房里的艺术空间，把一个喜欢的汉字、一句自己的想法画进涂鸦。与在北京结识的新伙伴共进欢送晚宴，介绍作品，回味一起经历的瞬间，也向让你感到亲切与安心的人认真说声谢谢。

Language Quest今日中文任务

My Two-City Street-Art MessageChoose a Chinese word or phrase that says something about your journey and make it part of your artwork. Explain your choice to a friend, then leave them a Chinese message about a moment or discovery you shared.

把双城故事写进作品选一个能代表这段旅程的中文词或一句话，把它画进作品。向朋友说说为什么选择它，再留下一句中文留言，记住共同经历的瞬间与发现。

At the table餐桌风味

A Western-Style Farewell with FriendsA relaxed Western-style dinner brings familiar flavours, warm dishes and vegetables to a table shared with your Beijing friends. Pass around stories and artwork, celebrate the days you spent together and make time for a proper goodbye.

欢送晚宴：西式伙伴餐桌轻松的西式餐桌上，熟悉的热餐与蔬菜陪着大家慢慢聊天。和北京的新老朋友分享作品、交换旅程里的趣事，在笑声里庆祝相识，也留出认真告别的时间。

DAY 16SUN27JUN

Beijing Goodbyes & the Journey Home

北京送机与温暖告别

Before departure离营前

Pack your Chinese notes and creations alongside memories of the people who shared these two cities with you. Say goodbye to teachers and friends, and choose a small thing to keep doing in Chinese at home—perhaps telling your family about someone you met along the way.

收好学习手册与中文作品，也记住一起走过双城的那些面孔。和老师、伙伴认真道别，想一件回家后还愿意用中文做的小事：也许先向家人讲一讲，这个夏天认识了怎样的朋友。

Departure arrangements离营安排

The RICH team arranges Beijing airport transfers around departure flights and helps everyone get ready for the journey. For campers leaving with a parent, teachers complete the handover in person, with time for a warm goodbye.

老师根据各自航班安排北京送机，陪大家做好离营准备；由家长来接的营员，当面完成交接。带着朋友的祝福与自己的双城故事，从容踏上回家的路。

Language Quest今日中文任务

Next Stop: Keep Speaking ChineseTake your two-city stories and learning work home. Next time you meet someone who speaks Chinese, try starting with a simple “Ni hao.”

下一站 中文继续带着双城故事与学习作品回家。下次遇见说中文的人，试着先说一声“你好”，让新的交流从这一句开始。

Departure meals离营餐食

Before We Say GoodbyeBreakfast and departure meals are arranged around flights and collection times, helping everyone leave camp ready for the journey ahead.

早餐与离营用餐根据接送与航班时间安排早餐及离营用餐，让大家吃好、准备好，再带着双城记忆踏上回家的路。

A thoughtfully planned summer · 关于这段旅程

This itinerary offers a picture of your child’s summer with RICH. We adapt the order to the weather, local arrangements and the pace of the group, while keeping the full programme of learning and experiences. There is room for the unexpected conversation or discovery that makes a day even richer.

这份行程，描绘的是孩子与RICH共度夏天的样子。我们会结合天气、现场安排和孩子们的状态调整活动顺序，保留完整的学习与体验。也为途中一场有趣的交谈、一个意外的发现留出空间，让真正发生的夏天，比纸上的行程更丰富。
